NFS-e Nacional · Odoo 19

Nota Fiscal de Serviço Eletrônica pelo Sistema Nacional, dentro do Odoo

Brazilian service invoices (NFS-e) through the national system (www.nfse.gov.br), from any service company on Odoo 19. Start in assisted mode with no certificate, and switch to direct emission through the API when the company's A1 certificate is at hand.

What it does

Two ways to issue

  • Assisted mode, no certificate: every value the Emissor Nacional website asks for is laid out with a copy button (customer, service code, amount, ISS, description); paste back the access key and the note is recorded.
  • Direct mode: the DPS is built by the national layout, checked against the official XSD (layout 1.01) with readable messages, signed (XMLDSig) with the company's ICP-Brasil A1 certificate and sent to SEFIN Nacional over mutual TLS. The authorised NFS-e XML stays on the note.
  • Test environment (produção restrita) and production.

After issuing

  • DANFSe drawn from the authorised XML (NT 008), with the QR code of the public consultation.
  • Cancellation event with its reason.
  • A lost answer is recovered instead of issuing the note twice (E0014).
  • Regimes: Simples Nacional, MEI and the others, with ISS withholding.

Customers filled in for you

  • Type the CNPJ and the company comes from the Receita Federal register: legal name, address with number and district, CEP, city with its IBGE code, state, phone and e-mail. Numeric and the new alphanumeric CNPJ (July 2026).
  • Type the CEP and the street, district, city, state and IBGE code fill in.
  • Everything stays editable, and a button brings the Receita data again later.

Works on its own

  • No dependency on Accounting: notes are made by hand, or from other modules.
  • With Oficina OS and Oficina OS – NFS-e, a work order or a monthly closing becomes a note with one click.
  • English source and full Brazilian Portuguese.

Good to know before installing

Outside services, and what is sent

  • Sistema Nacional NFS-e (SEFIN Nacional): in direct mode, when you issue or cancel a note, or recover the answer to one.
  • BrasilAPI, or Minha Receita when it does not answer: only the CNPJ you type, to read the public register.
  • ViaCEP: only the CEP you type.
  • No data is sent to the author, and there is no tracking.

Requirements and changes

  • Odoo 19, Community or Enterprise, on your own server or on Odoo.sh (not Odoo Online).
  • Python: cryptography, lxml, qrcode and requests, all shipped with Odoo 19.
  • Your municipality must be on the Sistema Nacional NFS-e. Check the service code, the ISS and the regime with your accountant, and issue in the test environment first.
  • Odoo's paid Partner Autocomplete leaves the Tax ID field, which the free CNPJ lookup now fills; it stays on the name.

Em português

Emita a NFS-e do padrão nacional direto do Odoo 19. Comece no modo assistido, sem certificado: o sistema organiza cada valor para copiar no Emissor Nacional. Com o certificado A1, emita direto pela API, com validação pelo XSD oficial, assinatura, DANFSe, cancelamento e recuperação de resposta perdida. Clientes preenchidos pelo CNPJ (cadastro da Receita Federal) e pelo CEP, de graça. Serve para qualquer empresa de serviços.
