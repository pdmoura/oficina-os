Oficina OS · Odoo 19

Work orders for fleet workshops, run from the mechanic's phone

Built with a truck electrical and air-conditioning shop in Brazil. The mechanic receives the truck by its plate, adds services, photos and the checklist on the phone; the office follows the yard on a dashboard, sends the quote by WhatsApp, the customer approves it by link with a signature, and each fleet is billed once a month.

Try the live demo Office: escritorio / escritorio · Mechanic: mecanico / mecanico · the first visit of the day takes about a minute to wake up

What it does

Mechanic app on the phone

  • Full-screen app, installable on the home screen (PWA), light or dark theme.
  • Receive a truck by its plate (Mercosul and old format): a known truck brings its customer and odometer; a new one is registered on the spot, and so is a new customer.
  • Services and customers picked from searchable lists, so a catalogue of hundreds stays quick.
  • Stages and bays with one tap, with the time spent in each stage.
  • Photos grouped by moment (arrival, job, delivery), shrunk on the phone before sending.
  • Arrival and delivery checklists answered item by item.
  • Mistakes are fixed on the phone: edit the order, delete one opened by mistake, undo "job ready".

Office

  • Dashboard of the day: trucks in the shop, late orders, quotes waiting, trucks ready, revenue of the month.
  • Order board by stage, drag and drop, and Odoo's own forms adapted to phones.
  • Approve, reject, cancel and reopen: office-only steps enforced on the server, not only hidden.
  • Services and parts on the same order, with labour and parts totals apart.
  • Work order PDF with the shop's logo, photos optional.
  • Guided tour for the office and for the mechanics, on computers and phones.

Customer approval by link

  • One link per order, sent by WhatsApp from the order.
  • The customer sees the truck, the services, the photos of the job and the total, picks the items and signs on the phone. No login.
  • Fleets on contract can have their orders approved automatically.

Monthly closing per fleet

  • Loads the customer's finished orders of the period, confirms and prints one report: one line per service and price, parts in their own table, the list of orders.
  • Ready for a service invoice: add NFS-e Nacional and Workshop Orders: NFS-e to issue it with one click.

Your brand

  • Logos for light and dark screens, colours, login page, browser icon, installed-app icon and link preview, per company.
  • Warranty and approval texts, stages, bays, sectors, services and checklists are data you edit.

Languages

  • English source and full Brazilian Portuguese, including terms Odoo 19 leaves untranslated.

Good to know before installing

What installing changes in Odoo

  • Brand colours apply to the whole backend (menus, buttons, forms), set in Settings.
  • The Workshop app comes first and opens on the dashboard for the office and on the mechanic app for mechanics.
  • Companies still on Odoo's defaults get Brazil, BRL and São Paulo time.
  • Self sign-up on the login page is closed once (the office creates the accounts); you can open it again in Settings.
  • OdooBot's onboarding chat is turned off, as it covers the phone screen.
  • The login page, browser tab and installed app use the shop's name, logo and colours.

Data and outside services

  • No data is sent to the author, and there is no tracking.
  • Photos are kept in your Odoo database. Optionally, in your own Cloudinary account: the phone uploads with a signature made by your server, and nothing else is shared.
  • WhatsApp buttons only open a wa.me link on the user's device.
  • The customer page shows one order to whoever has its link (a long random token) and records the name and signature the customer gives when approving.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19, Community or Enterprise, on your own server or on Odoo.sh. Odoo Online (SaaS) does not accept third-party modules with Python code.
  • No extra Python packages.

Em português

Ordens de serviço para oficinas de frotas (elétrica, ar-condicionado, diesel), feitas junto com uma oficina de caminhões no Brasil. O mecânico recebe o caminhão pela placa, lança serviços, peças, fotos e o checklist no celular; o escritório acompanha o pátio no painel, manda o orçamento pelo WhatsApp, o cliente aprova pelo link com assinatura, e cada frota é faturada uma vez por mês, pronta para a NFS-e.

Tudo em português, com a sua marca. Veja a demonstração: oficina-os.onrender.com (escritório: escritorio / escritorio, mecânico: mecanico / mecanico).
