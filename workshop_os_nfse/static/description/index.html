Oficina OS · NFS-e

The NFS-e of a work order or a monthly closing, with one click

Connects Oficina OS to NFS-e Nacional Brasil. It installs by itself when both are installed.

From the order or the closing

  • Issue NFS-e on a finished work order, or on a confirmed monthly closing of a fleet.
  • The note comes filled: customer, amount, competence and an itemised description, one line per service and price with the total.
  • Parts stay out: they are billed as goods, so the note covers labour only.

No double invoicing

  • An order or a closing has one live note at a time; cancel it to issue again.
  • A note started by hand can pick the order or the closing and fills itself in.
  • The closing turns Invoiced when its note is issued.
